---
title: Learning a Route
license: ai
---
The first time you walk a new route, every corner asks for attention. You remember the yellow door, the loud crossing, and the tree with roots pushing up the pavement.

After a while, the route becomes part of you. Your feet know when to turn, and your thoughts are free to travel somewhere else.

You begin to notice smaller things: which garden smells of mint, which window always has music behind it, and where the pavement stays wet after rain.

A familiar route is not boring. It is a shelf where memory places tiny objects, one after another, until the walk becomes a map of ordinary days.

Then, when you take the route after a long absence, it greets you strangely. Some details have changed, but your body still remembers the shape of the way.
