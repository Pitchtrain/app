---
title: Rain at the Window
license: ai
---
Rain changes the sound of a room. It softens the edges of traffic and gives every pause a little rhythm. Even ordinary objects seem more patient in rainy weather.

A cup waits on the table, a book lies open, and the window keeps the whole afternoon at a comfortable distance.

Outside, people walk faster, shoulders raised, umbrellas leaning into the wind. Inside, time feels slower, as if the room has decided to breathe more deeply.

There is comfort in hearing the weather without needing to enter it. The rain speaks in taps and streams, and each sound makes the silence around it easier to notice.

When the clouds finally lift, the street shines with reflected light. The room returns to itself, but a little of the rainy calm remains.
