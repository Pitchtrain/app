---
title: A Quiet Morning
license: ai
---
The street was still quiet when the first bakery opened its door. A soft smell of bread moved through the cool air, and the windows slowly caught the morning light.

For a few minutes, the city felt gentle and unhurried. Someone swept the sidewalk, a cyclist passed without speaking, and the day began without any need to announce itself.

At the corner, a delivery truck stopped with a soft hiss of brakes. The driver stacked crates beside a cafe door and nodded to a woman walking a small dog in a red coat.

By the time the first bus arrived, the spell was already changing. More footsteps joined the pavement, more voices crossed the street, and the quiet morning became an ordinary one.

Still, the memory of those first minutes stayed behind like warmth in a cup. It made the rest of the day feel a little less sharp.
