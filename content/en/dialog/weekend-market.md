Have you been to this market before?

Yes, but only once. There are more stands today.

The fruit looks fresh. I might buy some strawberries.

Good idea. The bakery stand is also very popular.

Do they sell bread or mostly cakes?

Both. Their rye bread is excellent, and the lemon cake is dangerous.

Dangerous sounds promising.

It is. I bought a slice last week and immediately wanted another one.

Let's look around first, then come back.

Perfect. I also need tomatoes and maybe some flowers.

Flowers would make the table look cheerful.

Exactly. A small weekend luxury.
